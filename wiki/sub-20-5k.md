# Sub-20 min 5k

## Goal
Break 20 minutes for 5k (4:00/km). Target: ~12 weeks from 2026-09-14 (i.e. by ~2026-12-07). Current 5k PB is 22:09 (4:26/km, Strava, ~Aug 2026 — not in Fitbit). Fitbit now covers all runs going forward. Gap to sub-20 is ~2 minutes. Review at week 6 — if interval paces are comfortable, plan can be accelerated.

## Approach
3 runs per week:
- **Quality session** (Tue or Wed): intervals or tempo. Main adaptation driver.
- **Long easy run** (Fri): 10–14 km at 5:30–6:00/km. Social run with friends counts here — pace and distance may vary.
- **Moderate/easy run** (Sun): 5–8 km easy.

3 gym sessions per week alongside (2 upper, 1 lower). Gym days sit between run days where possible.

**Flexibility rule**: runs with friends that stray from the plan are fine. When that happens, the rest of the week reshapes around what was actually done to still hit the week's training goal — never double up or skip recovery.

## 12-week programme

Each week: one quality run, one long easy run, one moderate/easy run. Gym (2 upper, 1 lower) sits in the gaps. All easy/long paces: 5:30–6:00/km. Social runs with friends slot into whichever run they best match; the remainder of the week reshapes around them.
